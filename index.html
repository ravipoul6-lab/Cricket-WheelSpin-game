T20 Spin Wheel

Wheel ghumao, international T20 stars pakdo, apni 8-stat team banao aur 2 se 6 doston ko harao!

Dost ne room code bheja hai? Yahan daalo:

Kaise khelein

  1. Host room banata hai aur link share karta hai. 2 se 6 players join kar sakte hain — sab aa jaayein to host game shuru karta hai.
  2. Shuru se pehle har player chupke se ek stat ko 2x chunta hai.
  3. Baari-baari SPIN karo. Jo player mile, uske stats sirf tumhe dikhenge — ek khaali slot mein lock karo ya Skip karo (poore game mein sirf 3 Skips).
  4. Faisle ke liye sirf 30 second! Time khatam hote hi player kisi bhi khaali slot mein apne aap lock ho jayega.
  5. DOUBLE SPIN aaye to turant ek aur spin, baari nahi badlegi.
  6. Sab ke 8 slots bhar gaye to har stat ka sabse behtar value 1 point (2x slot pe 2 points). Barabari pe sabse behtar sab ko point. Bowling avg aur Economy mein kam value behtar.
  7. Har game mein 150+ stars ke pool se naye players wheel pe aate hain.
